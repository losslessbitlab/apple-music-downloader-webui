Skip music videos

Drops standalone music-video URLs from your queue and excludes MV tracks from album / playlist downloads. AMD (the underlying tool) tends to get stuck on a region-locked or unavailable music video and stop the whole album, so leaving this ON is the safe default.

Turn it OFF only if you specifically want music-video files saved alongside the audio. Skipped MVs are reported in the queue card's log.

Auto-verify (deep probe)

After every scan, ping Apple's CDN once per quality tier to confirm what they'll actually serve. Format chips switch from ~ inferred to ✓ verified, and tiers Apple won't deliver are greyed out so you don't queue a job that'll fail mid-download.

Adds ~1–3 s per scanned URL. Recommended ON. Turn OFF for slightly faster scans (chips will say inferred only) — you can still hit Verify availability manually inside the scan modal.
