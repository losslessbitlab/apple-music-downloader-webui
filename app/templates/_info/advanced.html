Advanced — ports, MV options & ALAC Fix

Wrapper ports

decrypt-m3u8-port and get-m3u8-port are the TCP endpoints the bundled wrapper exposes to AMD. Defaults are 127.0.0.1:10020 and 127.0.0.1:20020; only touch them if another local service collides on those ports.

M3U8 Mode

  • Hi-Res Only — hits the on-device m3u8 endpoint only for tracks tagged Hi-Res. Faster scans, identical results for non-Hi-Res content.
  • All — queries the device endpoint for every track. Required if you want the absolute best variant Apple ever exposed, even for non-Hi-Res tracks. Slightly slower.

MV Audio Type / Max

Applies only when you download a music video directly. MV Audio Type picks the audio track inside the MV container: Atmos (best for Atmos gear), AC3 (Dolby Digital 5.1, broad compatibility), or AAC (stereo, smallest). MV Max is the maximum video height; 2160 caps at 4K, 1080 at FHD. Most catalogue MVs cap out at 1080p anyway.

ALAC Fix

Some early-2020s ALAC streams from Apple's CDN had malformed packet headers that confuse strict decoders (ffmpeg with -err_detect explode, foobar2000 1.x). Enable this to patch the packets post-download. Free to leave on; only matters when you hit one of those streams.

Get M3U8 from Device

Routes m3u8 manifest fetches through the bundled wrapper instead of the public web API. Slightly more reliable for Hi-Res tier discovery; required for some 192 kHz albums where the web API doesn't advertise the top tier.
