What do AAC-LC, ALAC, Atmos actually deliver?

AAC variants — when to pick which

Variant Bitrate ~MB/album Use it for
aac-lc256 kbps CBR~90 MBDefault. Apple Music's stock catalog tier; transparent to most listeners on most gear.
aac256 kbps VBR~80–100 MBSlightly more efficient encoder; sounds the same as AAC-LC, file size varies with content.
aac-binaural256 kbps~90 MBHeadphone-only mix derived from Atmos. Sounds "wider" on wired earbuds; weird on speakers.
aac-downmix256 kbps~90 MBStereo downmix of Atmos. Use when you want the Atmos mix "flattened" for non-Atmos gear.

AAC-LC requires a valid Media User Token. The other three are derived tiers and can fail with Unavailable on tracks Apple doesn't serve in that form — the queue will fall through your format chain in that case.

ALAC sample-rate caps — is 192 kHz worth it?

Cap Typical bitrate ~MB/album Realistically audible on…
44.1 kHz / 16-bit~950 kbps~330 MBAny setup. CD-quality. Indistinguishable from higher tiers on phones, AirPods, BT speakers.
48 kHz / 24-bit~1.5 Mbps~520 MBRecommended default. Same audible spectrum as 44.1, but 24-bit gives quieter noise floor on a real DAC + headphones.
96 kHz / 24-bit~3 Mbps~1.0 GBStudio monitors / IEMs above ~€500 + external DAC. Differences are measurement-grade, not casual-listen-grade.
192 kHz / 24-bit~6 Mbps~2.0 GBArchival only. Humans top out at ~20 kHz; 192 kHz captures content above 48 kHz, which only matters for downsampling math, not ears.

Rule of thumb: 48 kHz / 24-bit is the sweet spot for everyone who isn't a mastering engineer. Bump to 96/192 only if you have the gear and the disk; the file is twice as big for ear-untraceable headroom.

Atmos bitrates

Apple serves Dolby Atmos as a Joint-Object-Coded (JOC) stream. 2768 kbps is the highest tier available and what you want for an actual Atmos setup or Atmos-aware headphones. 2448 kbps is the fallback when 2768 isn't published for that release. Most albums don't have Atmos at all — the queue will fall back to your next format chain entry in that case.

Download Limit

Maximum number of tracks in a single album / playlist job. Bigger albums get truncated, not failed. Keep at 200 unless you regularly hit larger compilations.
